PROUST QUESTIONNAIRE

What is your idea of perfect happiness?
There is no such thing. However, I'm happy every day and as a general perception of life. I'm most happy when I have control over my projects and my kids have control over their lives.

Which historical figure do you most identify with?
None.

Which living person do you most admire?
My daughter Aglika.

What is the trait you most deplore in yourself?
Compliance.

What is the trait you most deplore in others?
Mediocrity.

What is your favorite journey?
Back into my memories.

What do you consider the most overrated virtue?
Propriety.

On what occasion do you lie?
I'm afraid of lying. I never do.

Which living person do you most despise?
Despise? What does this mean?

Which words or phrases do you most overuse?
Words of politeness.

What is your greatest regret?
Of having complied with the communist regime in my native country.

If you could change one thing about yourself, what would it be?
My place of birth.

What do you consider your greatest achievement?
Professionally, I became a writer using English in my 50s. In my private life, I enjoy the love of all my six children - after two decades of disrupted family life - and they love each other, despite their being spread around the world.

If you were to die and come back as a person or thing, what do you think it would be?
Italy.

What is your most treasured possession?
My private time.

What do you regard as the lowest depth of misery?
Friendlessness.

What is the quality you most like in a man?
Tolerance.

What is the quality you most like in a woman?
The blend between femininity and masculine brain power.

What do you most value in your friends?
Loyalty. Fidelity.

Who are your favorite writers?
Dostoiewski, Tomas Mann, Proust, Hemingway, Markes, F. Scott Fitzgerald, and Isaiah Berlin.

Who are your heroes in real life?
Car racers, acrobats, high-mountain snow boarders.

What is it that you most dislike?
Disorder.

How would you like to die?
All things settled, and surrounded by my kids and beloved woman.

What is your motto?
"If I'm not for myself, who will be for me? If I'm for myself only, what am I? If not now - when?"
